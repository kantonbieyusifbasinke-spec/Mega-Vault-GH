Still need help? Contact us ↗
Which networks are supported?+

MEGA VAULT GH is designed for MTN, Telecel and AT Ghana. Available bundles depend on the packages enabled by the administrator.

Do I need an account to order?+

Yes. Sign in or create an account so your orders, wallet and support requests can be securely linked to you.

How do I know if my data order is complete?+

Open your account and check Order History. A live order is marked successful only after the configured provider confirms the transaction.

Can I pay with Mobile Money?+

Paystack and Hubtel checkout integrations are prepared for supported payment methods, including supported Mobile Money channels. Live checkout is enabled only after merchant credentials and verification are configured.

What happens if an order fails?+

For wallet-funded live orders, a confirmed provider failure triggers a wallet refund. Unconfirmed provider responses remain pending while status checks are retried.
